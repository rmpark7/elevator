Spatial Protocol Project

An interactive space design experiment that disrupts the assumptions of everyday environments and introduces unexpected gestures to engage and redirect people's attention.

BEFORE

Most passengers experience social awkwardness and a lack of visual direction. To avoid face-to-face interaction or communication, they tend to adopt a closed posture, keeping their eyes glued to their smartphone screens.

AFTER

This is the space after dismantling conventional spatial norms and introducing the "Gesture Protocol."
The dynamic design of the red piggy bank and the "A+ Coins" applied to the elevator glass provides passengers with a fresh sense of stimulation and delight. The environment transforms into an interactive space where people’s eyes naturally follow the movement of the elevator, effectively drawing everyone's focus.
